# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements, workflows, and acceptance details. They help teams align scope, assumptions, and success criteria before work moves into delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and clarify business requirements, constraints, and dependencies
- Document process flows, requirements, and acceptance details
- Identify gaps, ambiguities, and scope risks early
- Support backlog refinement with clear, testable inputs

### Goals
- Reduce rework caused by unclear requirements
- Improve traceability from stakeholder need to delivered outcome
- Strengthen accountability around scope and acceptance decisions

### Typical Communication
- Requirement walkthroughs with Product Managers and Project Managers (PMs)
- Clarification sessions with Developers and QA/Testing
- Stakeholder interviews, notes, and follow-up summaries

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Partner with Product Managers to turn business context into prioritized, actionable backlog items
- Work with Developers to clarify edge cases, dependencies, and implementation assumptions
- Support Project Managers (PMs) by surfacing scope risks, decisions, and dependency impacts
- Align with QA/Testing on acceptance criteria so validation matches the intended outcome
- Confirm stakeholder expectations, terminology, and approvals before major scope commitments

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers define the user experience, interaction patterns, and interface details needed to deliver usable solutions. They help ensure customer needs are reflected in both workflow design and implementation handoff.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, wireframes, mockups, and interaction guidance
- Validate usability assumptions with Product Managers and stakeholders
- Document design intent, states, and edge cases for handoff
- Collaborate on accessibility and consistency across the experience

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and adoption
- Reduce ambiguity during design-to-development handoff
- Ensure the delivered experience matches user needs and product goals

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers, Developers, and QA/Testing
- Handoff notes and annotated mocks for delivery teams
- Feedback sessions with stakeholders and pilot users when needed

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to align design choices with user problems, outcomes, and priorities
- Collaborate with Developers to confirm feasibility, implementation details, and interaction behavior
- Keep Project Managers (PMs) informed on design dependencies, review timing, and handoff readiness
- Provide QA/Testing with expected behaviors and visual references for validation
- Incorporate stakeholder feedback while helping maintain focus on user needs and agreed scope

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide implementation direction, architecture choices, and technical risk management. They help the team make sound engineering decisions while staying aligned with delivery goals.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design decisions and implementation approach
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Support estimation, sequencing, and trade-off discussions
- Coach Developers through design reviews and complex delivery work

### Goals
- Maintain technical quality and delivery predictability
- Reduce architecture and integration risk
- Keep implementation choices aligned with product and project constraints

### Typical Communication
- Technical planning with Developers and Product Managers
- Risk and dependency reviews with Project Managers (PMs)
- Architecture notes, design reviews, and escalation guidance

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Advise Product Managers on technical trade-offs, feasibility, and sequencing impacts
- Partner with Developers on design patterns, code quality, and implementation decisions
- Support Project Managers (PMs) with realistic estimates, technical risk visibility, and escalation paths
- Align with QA/Testing on test strategy for complex flows, integrations, and critical risks
- Explain technical constraints and solution options to stakeholders in decision-ready terms

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the activities required to ship changes safely and predictably. They focus on release readiness, deployment communication, and post-release follow-through across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release checklists, calendars, and readiness criteria
- Coordinate deployment timing, approvals, and rollback planning
- Confirm dependencies, documentation, and communication are ready for launch
- Track post-release issues and follow-up actions

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and last-minute surprises
- Improve coordination across launch activities
- Ensure releases are communicated, verified, and closed out clearly

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness reviews with Project Managers (PMs), Developers, and QA/Testing
- Launch plans and go/no-go updates for stakeholders
- Post-release summaries, incident follow-ups, and action tracking

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Work with Product Managers to align release timing with business priorities and launch expectations
- Coordinate with Developers on deployment steps, rollback plans, and production support coverage
- Partner with Project Managers (PMs) to manage release dependencies, approvals, and status communication
- Confirm with QA/Testing that validation, defect triage, and release sign-off criteria are complete
- Provide stakeholders with clear launch status, risks, and post-release outcomes

---

## Support/Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support/Operations Representatives bring production readiness, customer-impact awareness, and operational feedback into project delivery. They help teams prepare for real-world usage and respond effectively after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share production support considerations, known pain points, and readiness needs
- Review monitoring, alerting, runbooks, and support documentation
- Surface customer issues and operational trends that should influence planning
- Participate in post-release verification and incident follow-up

### Goals
- Improve operational readiness before release
- Shorten the feedback loop from production back into planning
- Reduce customer impact from preventable issues

### Typical Communication
- Readiness and incident reviews with Developers, Release Managers, and QA/Testing
- Feedback summaries for Product Managers and Project Managers (PMs)
- Stakeholder updates on support trends or operational concerns when needed

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Inform Product Managers about customer pain points, support trends, and operational implications of roadmap choices
- Work with Developers to improve observability, supportability, and incident response readiness
- Keep Project Managers (PMs) aware of operational risks, readiness blockers, and post-release action items
- Partner with QA/Testing on production-like validation scenarios and defect severity context
- Help stakeholders understand support impacts, adoption issues, and stabilization needs after launch
